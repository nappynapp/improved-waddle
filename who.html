Robo Frostor is the 5th and last member to debut in the Korean VTuber group Skoshism on June 30th, 2024, being the stoic, quiet type in the group (but the more you watch him, the more you will see he's the exact opposite of stoic.)

He's in charge of the doing a lot of the creative work on his team, especially in music. He has knowledge in mixing, mastering and editing music along with enough knowledge in singing to be in charge of being the vocal and harmony guide/director for some of the group's songs.
